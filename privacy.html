Privacy

Summary: we upload nothing. Ever.

The short version: your photos are processed on your device and never uploaded. Your birth data is computed on your device and never uploaded. The only thing that ever reaches us is an email address — and only if you type it in yourself.

Face scans

When you scan your face, the image is analyzed entirely within your browser, on your device, using an on-device machine-learning model. Video frames and uploaded photos exist only in your device's temporary memory:

  • We do not upload, transmit, store, or back up any photo or video frame.
  • The camera stream is stopped the moment your scan completes.
  • An uploaded photo is discarded immediately after your device extracts facial measurements.
  • What remains is a handful of geometric ratios (like face length-to-width) used to show your result — not an image.

Birth data

Your date, time, and place of birth are used to calculate your BaZi chart locally in your browser. This information is not transmitted to us and is not stored on any server. If you close the page, it is gone.

Email addresses

If you choose to join the waitlist or save your chart, we receive the email address you provide. We use it only to send you what you asked for (product updates, or your saved chart). You can ask us to delete it at any time by replying to any email or writing to privacy@mien.app.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting, aggregate analytics to understand how the product is used (for example, how many scans are completed). These tools do not receive your photos, birth data, or email address.

Biometric data (US Illinois BIPA & similar laws)

Some laws (such as Illinois's Biometric Information Privacy Act) treat facial geometry as biometric information. Because all facial analysis occurs locally on your device, and Mien does not collect, capture, purchase, receive through download, or otherwise obtain any biometric identifiers or information, no biometric data ever reaches Mien or any third party. There is nothing to delete, because nothing is ever collected.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Since we never receive your photos or birth data, there is generally nothing for us to export or delete on your behalf. For the email address you voluntarily provide, you may request access, correction, or deletion at any time by writing to privacy@mien.app. We respond within 30 days.

Children

Mien is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Entertainment disclaimer

Mien is a product for entertainment and self-discovery, rooted in traditional Chinese physiognomy and BaZi astrology. It is not medical, psychological, legal, or financial advice, and it does not diagnose, treat, or predict anything.
